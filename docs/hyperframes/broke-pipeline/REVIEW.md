# Review notes

## Creative decisions

- The film explains the actual input pipeline rather than inventing a product UI.
- A near-black canvas, neutral type, fine grid, and one mint accent keep the look
  aligned with a developer tool rather than a launch campaign.
- The conversation grows first; only then does the processor reveal Clean, Trim,
  and optional local Ollama summarization.
- The first request and recent turns visibly bypass compression. A persistent
  lower lane shows that stored task history remains unchanged.
- No token, cost, or percentage claim appears in the film. The output is labeled
  as measured before/after without implying a universal saving.

## Review evidence

- Duration: 10 seconds.
- Delivery MP4: 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps.
- GitHub GIF: 1920 × 1080, 150 frames over 10 seconds, infinite loop.
- Full Hyperframes check: zero lint, runtime, layout, motion, contrast, or craft
  findings across seven key timestamps; 66 of 66 contrast checks passed.
- Visual review covered five narrative beats and a 360 px-wide contact sheet.

## Weakest frame

The opening second is intentionally sparse while the first pieces of context
arrive. It is the least information-dense frame, but the short pause makes the
later pipeline reveal easier to understand and gives the loop a clean reset.

## Known constraints

The detailed labels are designed for the GitHub README's desktop width. On a
narrow mobile viewport the overall before → pipeline → after story remains
legible, while the smallest annotations become supporting texture.
